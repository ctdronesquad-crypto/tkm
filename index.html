Who We Are

A movement, not a monument

Torchbearers Kingdom Movement exists to bring godly and spiritual enlightenment to this generation and beyond — actualizing the manifestation of the Kingdom of God on earth.

We accomplish this by impacting every sphere of society through the Biblical concept of the Seven Mountains of Influence — raising torchbearers who carry Kingdom light into education, government, business, family, health, media, and the church itself.

The Seven Mountains of Influence
