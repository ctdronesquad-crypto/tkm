The Seven Mountains

Occupying every sphere of society with Kingdom light.

The Seven Mountains framework is a biblical model for influence. TKM believes every part of society can be transformed through discipleship, leadership, and purposeful engagement.

Kingdom Influence

Seven Mountains of Influence

We are committed to shaping culture through education, family, government, business, media, health, and the church.

1. Education

Education

Raising minds that think with Kingdom wisdom and preparing leaders for every field of life.

“The future of a nation is shaped in the classroom.”
2. Religion

Religious Identity

Strengthening spiritual life, doctrine, worship, and authentic kingdom expression in the church and beyond.

“The church must shine with truth and holiness.”
3. Politics & Law

Politics & Legal Systems

Equipping people to influence governance with justice, righteousness, and godly leadership.

“Righteousness exalts a nation.”
4. Economy

Economic Identity

Building stewardship, enterprise, and financial wisdom that blesses communities and advances the Kingdom.

“Kingdom wealth should be a blessing to many.”
5. Family

Family Identity

Restoring the family as a place of love, order, purpose, and spiritual foundation.

“The family is the first institution of influence.”
6. Health

Health Identity

Promoting wholeness in body, soul, and spirit through compassion, care, and wisdom.

“Physical healing and spiritual vitality belong together.”
7. Entertainment & Media

Entertainment & Sports

Influencing culture through media, arts, sports, and creative expression that reflects Kingdom values.

“Culture is shaped by what people celebrate.”
