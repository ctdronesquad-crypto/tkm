Prayer

We stand with you in prayer and intercession.

Whether you are facing a challenge, carrying a burden, or seeking direction, we welcome your prayer request and will stand with you in faith.

Submit a prayer request

Prayer is powerful, and the Lord hears every cry. Share your need and we will lift it before God in faith and unity.

  • Health and healing
  • Family and relationships
  • Financial breakthrough
  • Spiritual direction and peace
  • Protection, restoration, and breakthrough
Send Prayer Request on WhatsApp Back to Home

You can also send your prayer request directly through WhatsApp for a quick and personal response.

What to include

  • Your name (or a preferred name)
  • A short description of the request
  • Any urgent need or prayer focus
  • How we can pray with you
